🌐 Custom Domain (ChatLink)
Use your own subdomain like chat.yourbusiness.com
Point a CNAME record from your subdomain to chat.quicbizz.app in your DNS settings. After saving, we'll verify and activate it (may take up to 48 hours).
DNS Setup Instructions
Type: CNAME
Name: chat (or your chosen subdomain prefix)
Value: chat.quicbizz.app
TTL: 3600 (or Auto)
Works with GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, and all major DNS providers.
